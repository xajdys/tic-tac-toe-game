Hi there! Welcome to the Tic Tac Toe Game

Here are the rules of the game:

  • Player 1 will be X
  • Player 2 will be O
  • Players take turns placing their marks on the board
  • The first player to get 3 of their marks in a row (up, down, across, or diagonally) wins
  • If all 9 squares are filled and no player has won, the game is a draw
